# Slidezz

- Logo mark: `frontend/assets/slidezz-mark.png`
- Horizontal logo: `frontend/assets/slidezz-logo.png`
- Colors: emerald `#16745C`, coral `#F07167`, dark ink `#263631`.

The mark combines stacked slides with a forward chevron. The app uses native text
for an accessible wordmark, and standalone HTML embeds its mark and favicon.
Both PNG assets are transparent and generated with the built-in image_gen tool.
